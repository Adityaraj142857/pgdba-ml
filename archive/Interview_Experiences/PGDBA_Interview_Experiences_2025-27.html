Tips for Aspirants
  • Analyse previous year question papers carefully — certain areas consistently carry high difficulty levels. Focus more on those areas while preparing. Remember, it’s not during practice but in the exam when those 35 questions from QA and LRDI feel overwhelming due to the pressure and time crunch. That’s when your preparation truly gets tested. So, go beyond standard practice — solve slightly tougher questions, even from JEE Main level, to build that extra edge.
  • Also, for interviews, make sure to prepare from the pgdba.ml website — it has a good collection of previous year interview questions. Go through them thoroughly to get a clear idea of what kind of questions are usually asked. This will help you stay one step ahead and reduce the surprise factor during the actual interview.
  • Confidence is built not just by accuracy, but by being comfortable under pressure and knowing the terrain well — both in the exam and in the interview And make sure that u put u r effort at the right areas
