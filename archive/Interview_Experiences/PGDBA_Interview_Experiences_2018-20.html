Questions asked in PGDBA interview along with your answers.
There were three interviewers on the panel .I was asked to tell me something about myself and what I want to do next (probably an icebreaker). While I was doing that, they read my CV and asked me about my 3-month internship in Taiwan and the work that I did there. Following that up, they asked me about one other project in depth that I did in Physics. (They didn't ask me about other projects that were mentioned in the CV which were in relevant fields, like Data structures and another one in Analytics). Then came the part around which majority of the interview revolved. The professor in the middle said ,so you are from IIT Delhi. I should assume you have amazing skills in Mathematics.I said you can say that. The professor sitting on the left asked me about double derivatives, maxima-minima, limits, continuity of 4-5 different functions, each time giving a more complex function and tried to test whether I knew what I was saying and asked me to draw a few functions. But the questions were quite basic and he seemed happy with the answers. *Over to the professor in the middle*. He asked me if my degree had a probability course. I told him that it didn't but I did a course in Probability and Stochastic processes for extra credits in third year. He asked me to write the probability density function, the cumulative density function of exponential distribution and then draw both of them. When I did that, he asked me what a memoryless process is. I told him that the future state of the process depends on the current state of the process and not the past states . He said that is not the description of a memoryless process. I tried to make him understand in a few different ways but he said no. Then the professor wrote a differential equation that I had never seen in my life. He asked me what it was. I could only answer that it was a linear homogeneous differential equation because beyond that I had no idea. He said it is a linear homogeneous differential equation but what is the name of the equation. I looked at it and tried to remember but in vain. Then he said, it must have been taught to you in the first year. I said it wasn't taught. He said, it is simply impossible. You need to go back home and look at your notes. I couldn't argue as it would look bad on my part. *Already sinking in my chair* *Over to the professor on the right* He asked me that you have played basketball on a lot of levels as mentioned in your CV, tell me name of one player. I was relieved that he didn't ask the size of the court or the ball which I had mugged up the previous night.I said Kobe Bryant. He asked who is the greatest player of all time. I said Michael Jordan. He asked me to name one player playing in NBA who is from India. I answered Satnam Singh. Then he asked me to name one player from team India to which I answered Amritpal Singh and the interview was then over.The written test had more weightage as inspite of not performing very well in interviews, I was selected.
Any suggestion for aspirants regarding preparation for written test and interview.
Ace the written test and for the interview focus on everything that's in your CV very well
